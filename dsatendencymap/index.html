DSA Tendency Map

A map of the main caucuses in the Democratic Socialists of America, grouped by what each says about the ballot line, with how the National Political Committee (NPC) has actually voted on contested questions. It describes; it does not rate anyone's politics. Most DSA members belong to no caucus, though caucuses dominate internal politics.

● Confirmed: the caucus's own words, or consistent across reports ◐ Single source ○ Unverified or derived Last updated Oct 23, 2026

Select a caucus to see what it says, with sources.

Each block is a caucus, sized by its NPC seats (27 total: Groundwork 5, Bread & Roses 5, SMC 4, SoR 4, MUG 3, Red Star 3, R&R 2, LSC 1). Solid green = backed, solid orange = opposed, dashed grey = position not reported in the sources I found. Seat counts change; each vote used the NPC of its day.
