MILE 0The Start

Systems built from the ground up.

I lead regional operations across Sacramento, Solano, and Yolo counties for Bay Area Community Services. It's growth I started building in 2021 with a single crisis navigation program and 13 staff. That foundation has since grown into a full portfolio of programs, including our current Full Service Partnership (FSP) build, with 200+ staff across the region today and expansion planned past 250. Off the clock, I coach leaders through Cadence Leadership+Communication, and I currently serve as VP of the Association of Behavioral Health Contractors.
